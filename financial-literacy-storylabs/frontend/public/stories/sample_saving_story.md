# Emma's First Piggy Bank Adventure

*A Financial Literacy Story for Ages 4-6*

## Story Overview

**Learning Objectives:**
- Understanding what saving means
- Learning about earning money through helping
- Making choices between spending and saving
- Setting simple financial goals

**Characters:**
- Emma (6 years old, loves animals)
- Penny the Pig (magical piggy bank)
- Financial Guide (narrator)

---

## Scene 1: Meeting Penny the Pig

*Setting: Emma's colorful bedroom with a new pink piggy bank on the dresser*

**Financial Guide:** "Meet Emma, a curious 6-year-old who just received her very first piggy bank! It's pink with a big smile and ready to help her learn about money."

**Penny the Pig:** "Hello Emma! I'm Penny the Pig, and I'm here to help you learn about saving money! Do you know what saving means?"

**Emma:** "I think saving means keeping my money safe! But why would I want to do that?"

**Penny the Pig:** "That's exactly right! We save money so we can buy something special later. What's something you really want?"

**Emma:** "I want that big teddy bear I saw at the toy store!"

**Financial Guide:** "This is called a 'goal' - something we want to get or do in the future. Having a goal makes saving more fun!"

**Interactive Choice:** "What do you think Emma should do first to start saving for her teddy bear?"

---

## Scene 2: Earning Her First Coins

*Setting: Kitchen with Emma helping to set the table*

**Financial Guide:** "Emma's mom has some coins to give her, but first she needs to earn them by helping with chores."

**Emma:** "I can help! I can set the table and put my toys away!"

**Penny the Pig:** "Great job, Emma! When we work to get money, we call that 'earning.' You earned these coins by being helpful!"

**Emma:** "Wow! I have 3 shiny coins now!"

**Financial Guide:** "Emma now has 3 coins. She can choose what to do with them. What do you think she should do?"

**Interactive Choice:** "Should Emma save all her coins, or spend some and save some?"

---

## Scene 3: The Big Choice

*Setting: Outside the toy store window*

**Financial Guide:** "Emma walks by the toy store and sees a small toy for 2 coins. She could buy it now, or save all her coins for something bigger later."

**Emma:** "The toy looks fun, but I want to save for that big teddy bear I saw! What should I do?"

**Penny the Pig:** "This is a great thinking moment! If you buy the small toy now, you'll have 1 coin left. If you save all 3 coins, you'll be closer to your teddy bear goal!"

**Financial Guide:** "Both choices are okay! Sometimes we spend money on things we want now, and sometimes we save for bigger things later."

**Interactive Choice:** "What would you choose? Buy the small toy now, or save for the bigger goal?"

---

## Scene 4: Saving Success

*Setting: Emma's bedroom, putting coins in the piggy bank*

**Financial Guide:** "Emma decided to save her coins! She carefully puts all 3 coins into Penny the Pig."

**Penny the Pig:** "Hooray! You're such a smart saver, Emma! Your coins are safe with me, and they're growing toward your teddy bear goal!"

**Emma:** "I feel good about saving! Now I know that earning money and saving it helps me get things I really want!"

**Financial Guide:** "Emma learned two important things today: We can earn money by helping others, and we can save money to reach our goals."

**Interactive Choice:** "What goal would you like to save for?"

---

## What We Learned

**Financial Concepts Covered:**
1. **Saving** - Keeping money safe for future use
2. **Earning** - Getting money by doing helpful work
3. **Goal Setting** - Deciding what we want to save for
4. **Choice Making** - Deciding between spending now or saving for later

**Real-World Applications:**
- Ask parents about chores you can do to earn money
- Get your own piggy bank or savings container
- Think about something special you want to save for
- Practice making choices between small wants and bigger goals

**Discussion Questions:**
- What would you like to save money for?
- How could you earn money by helping at home?
- When might it be okay to spend money instead of saving?
- How does it feel to reach a savings goal?
